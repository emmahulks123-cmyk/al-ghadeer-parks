The premise

Sold on a number that never arrives.

Seven to ten percent. It is the yield on almost every brochure here, and it is gross. Quoted before service charges, before management, before a single week of vacancy.

What reaches your account is closer to four to six. That gap is not a scandal. It is arithmetic nobody selling you a unit is paid to perform.

Better you hear it from us at the start than find it in year two.
