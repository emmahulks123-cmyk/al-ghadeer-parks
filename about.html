The firm

Engaged by the buyer. Never the developer.

Bramwell & Partners is a boutique property advisory working across Dubai and Abu Dhabi, founded by principals with more than two decades between them in these two markets.

Why we exist

Almost everyone here is paid to sell you something.

In a market where commission flows from the developer, the incentive is to move the stock that pays best. That is not a scandal, it is simply how most of the industry is built. It does mean the advice you receive is rarely neutral.

We took the other structure. Our fee comes from the buyer, disclosed in writing before any work begins, and we take nothing from developers. It makes for a smaller business. It also makes our advice worth something, because there is no version of the transaction where we do better by pointing you at the wrong building.

The practical result is short shortlists, slow recommendations, and a written argument against every purchase we put in front of you.

The approach

Three commitments, held on every file.

01

Advisory, not inventory

We are not paid to move a particular building. We are paid to be right about the one you buy. That is why our shortlists are short.

02

Access earned over two decades

Our principals have worked Dubai and Abu Dhabi for more than twenty years. That opens certain doors, and closes the wrong ones early.

03

The full file, awkward parts included

Supply pipeline, handover record, service charge trajectory, exit liquidity. You get the case against the purchase alongside the case for it.

The process

Three steps. No viewings until the third.

01

The brief

One conversation about capital, horizon, residency intentions and genuine appetite for risk. We do not show you property in this meeting. Understanding what you are solving for comes first, and it saves months later.

02

The shortlist

Three to five options, rarely more. Each arrives with the net figure worked through, the supply picture for that specific community, the developer's delivery record, and where the residency threshold falls on your timeline.

03

The decision

We view, negotiate, structure and handle registration. If nothing on the list holds up under the final look, we tell you plainly and we wait. A year of patience has cost our clients far less than a wrong entry.

What you receive

Every recommendation shows its working.

No property reaches your shortlist without these six pieces of work behind it. You keep the file whether you buy through us or not.

01

Net yield model

Gross rent, the building's actual service charge rate, management, vacancy and the transfer fee, laid out year by year rather than flattened into one percentage.

02

Supply pipeline

What else completes within eighteen months of your handover, and what that volume tends to do to achievable rent. The analysis most often skipped.

03

Developer record

Past handover dates against promised ones, escrow standing and build quality on delivered stock. Track record is the most reliable predictor in off plan.

04

Residency position

Whether the purchase clears the AED 2 million Golden Visa threshold, and precisely when. Off plan and ready property behave very differently here.

05

Exit assessment

Resale liquidity in the community, typical days on market and the buyer profile you would be selling into. Entry is easy here. Exit is worth planning.

06

The case against

A written argument for not proceeding. If we cannot make one, that is itself a signal. Usually we can, and it is the page clients read twice.
